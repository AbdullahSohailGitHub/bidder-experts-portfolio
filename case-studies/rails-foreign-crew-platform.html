The Challenge

The client managed hundreds of foreign crew members across multiple vessels using a patchwork of spreadsheets, email threads and manual paperwork. This created compliance risks, scheduling conflicts and hours of admin work every week.

They needed a single, reliable platform to handle crew records, certifications, rotation scheduling and document expiry tracking — built to handle growth without breaking.

Our Approach

We built a custom Ruby on Rails application with a clean, role-based dashboard. Crew profiles, certifications and visa documents were centralised, with automated alerts for expiring documents.

A scheduling engine handled crew rotations and flagged conflicts before they happened. We added secure document storage, audit logging for compliance, and a reporting layer so management could see fleet-wide status at a glance.
