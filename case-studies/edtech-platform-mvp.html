The Challenge

The founder needed to validate their EdTech idea quickly but didn't have a technical team. They needed a complete, polished MVP — not a prototype — that could onboard real students and take payments from day one.

Speed mattered: they had a launch window and a limited budget, so every feature had to earn its place.

Our Approach

We scoped a focused MVP and built it full-stack. Students could browse courses, watch video lessons, take quizzes and track progress. Instructors got a dashboard to create and manage content.

We integrated secure payments for course purchases, built responsive layouts for mobile learners, and deployed on scalable cloud infrastructure — delivering a launch-ready product within the founder's timeline and budget.
