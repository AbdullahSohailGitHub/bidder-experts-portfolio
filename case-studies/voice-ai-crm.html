The Challenge

The client's team couldn't answer every inbound call, and leads that weren't followed up within minutes went cold. Manual data entry into their CRM was slow and error-prone, and after-hours calls were simply lost.

They wanted an AI voice agent that could handle calls naturally, capture lead details, and push everything into their CRM — around the clock.

Our Approach

We built a Voice AI agent using modern LLM and speech technology that answers calls, holds natural conversations, qualifies the caller, and books appointments. Every interaction is transcribed and logged.

The agent was integrated directly with the client's CRM via API, so new leads, call summaries and follow-up tasks were created automatically. We added workflow automation for instant SMS/email follow-up after each call.
