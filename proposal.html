The winning formula

1HookOpen with their exact problem, not your bio
→
2ProofOne sharp, relevant result or project
→
3PlanA short, clear path to the outcome
→
4CTAInvite an easy next step

✓ Do

  • Lead with the client's problem in the first line.
  • Mirror keywords from the job post.
  • Keep it skimmable — short paragraphs, one idea each.
  • Show one specific, relevant result.
  • End with a low-friction question.
  • Apply in the first hour while the post is fresh.

✗ Don't

  • Open with "I am a passionate developer with…".
  • Paste the same generic template every time.
  • List every skill you have ever touched.
  • Write a wall of text with no breaks.
  • Over-promise or undercut your rate to win.
  • Forget to actually answer their question.
